One number on a banner

  • The call is captured, de-duplicated and tagged with agent, village, crop and season
  • A Marathi message arrives within seconds, or the client can choose silent capture

Notes and edge cases

  1. 1One number per agent gives clean attribution. The number alone does not say crop or season, so events add that context (next screen).
  2. 2Transfers and exits. When an agent leaves or moves territory, past leads stay with the agent of record, new calls follow the new owner.
  3. 3Reuse risk. A farmer may call an old number weeks later. Parking period plus a fallback response avoids attributing the call to the wrong agent.
